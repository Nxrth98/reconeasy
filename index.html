Guide & policies

How to use ReconEasy

Quick start

1. Photograph the slip — as soon as you get it, snap it in Credit Card or Petty Cash. Don't wait for month-end.

2. Let the AI read it — merchant, amount and category are suggested automatically. Check them, then tap Apply.

3. Add the vote & project — search by name or number. If unsure, ask your project manager before saving.

4. Describe it in 60 characters — short and specific, e.g. "Diesel refuel for rental vehicle".

5. Save, then sync — entries stay on your device until you tap Sync in Export. That's what pushes them to Drive and Sheets.

Good to know

The green dot on an entry means it has a vote, project and description. Amber means it still needs attention before month-end.

Keep the original paper slip even after photographing it — the signed policies below still require the physical copy to reach Finance.

Before you spend

The card is for work purposes only and has a set monthly limit — get your manager's sign-off before spending, not after.

Personal expenses are never allowed on the card — this includes things like an Uber from home to your normal work base.

Issuing the card is a privilege, not a right — it can be withdrawn at any time, and unexplained spend is deducted from your salary.

First card? You'll need a clean background check as well as completed probation — any exception needs written CEO/COO approval.

What the card can't be used for

Toiletries and other personal items are off-limits on the card, even small ones.

Medical items like Panado or Disprin need pre-approval and a written reason before you buy them.

Buying alcohol — e.g. for hosting a donor — needs sign-off from a senior manager, director or the COO/CEO first.

Bought a small asset under R5,000 on the card, like a tool? Declare it to the Procurement Manager so it's added to the insurance and asset registers.

Subscriptions like ChatGPT or Adobe need pre-approval from a senior manager, director or the COO/CEO before you sign up.

Vehicle repairs or servicing can only go on the card once the Fleet Manager has signed off, and the invoice must show the vehicle's registration number.

Catering or event invoices need the event date and headcount shown, plus sign-off from your line manager and budget holder.

Keeping it secure

Never share your PIN, and never let the card out of sight when it's being swiped.

Never send the full 16-digit number or CVV by email or WhatsApp — only the last 4 digits, and only when Finance needs them.

Watch your transaction SMSes closely — report anything unusual to the bank immediately, then to your finance manager.

Card lost or stolen? Call Investec on 0860 110 161 (or +27 11 286 9663 abroad) immediately to stop it, then get a police case number and tell your finance manager.

Falsifying receipts, inflating amounts or claiming personal spend as Trust spend is treated as misconduct under the Trust's disciplinary process.

Slips & deadlines

Original slips and vouchers are due to Finance by the 6th of every month for the previous month's statement.

A speedpoint slip alone isn't enough — you need the actual invoice or till slip too, or it will be rejected.

Travelling internationally? Contact Investec at least two weeks ahead — they'll need your passport, ticket and travel details.

Paying a tip? Write the actual tip amount on the slip.

Going over the R500 monthly speedpoint-slip limit needs a written motivation approved by the CEO or their delegate.

Lost your slips to theft? You'll need a police affidavit for the finance team, or the amount may be recovered from your salary.

Repeated missed deadlines or holding a large, unexplained petty cash balance can lead to your card being suspended.

Before you spend

Get your manager's advance approval for petty cash spend, same as the card — plan the project cost before you spend, not after.

No personal expenses — the same home-to-work Uber rule applies to cash as it does to the card.

New to holding petty cash? A background check is required first, and you'll be issued your own petty cash vote number.

Limits

Petty cash draws shouldn't exceed R20,000 a month — anything more needs your line manager's motivation, reviewed by Finance.

A single petty cash transaction is capped at R5,000. Going over that needs written manager approval, reviewed by Finance.

No spend this month? Submit a blank recon anyway — even if you only drew cash and didn't use it.

Getting a valid slip

A till slip or invoice is required at the point of sale — it's on you to make sure you're given one.

Handwritten invoices are allowed, but only if they include: supplier's name & address, WILDTRUST as the buyer (plus VAT numbers if vatable), a 10-digit contact number, an ID or registration number, the date, what was bought, and a signature or stamp.

No slip available at all (e.g. informal transport)? Get a written, signed note from the provider with their contact details, and countersign it yourself.

Affidavits for a missing slip are only accepted for theft or genuinely exceptional cases — not for a slip you simply misplaced.

Keep descriptions to 60 characters, but always cover what was bought, the event or workshop, and who it was for.

Deadlines & balances

Your recon, signed off by your manager, is due to Finance by the 6th of every month.

Passing petty cash to a colleague? Both of you sign a petty cash receipt, and it must go in with your monthly recon.

Holding more than R2,000 in cash can be flagged for review — keep your balance reconciled and don't let it build up.

Missing a slip? You get about 45 days to track it down before the amount is recovered from your salary.

Recurring payments, like a monthly cleaning fee, can't go through petty cash more than once — repeat suppliers must be registered vendors instead (grocers and restaurants are the exception).

Petty cash can't be deposited into your personal bank account, except in pre-approved cases for remote-area work.

Registered Trust vendors can't be paid via petty cash without written sign-off from the Finance Director or CEO.

Bought a small asset under R5,000 with petty cash, like a tool? Declare it to the Procurement Manager for the insurance and asset registers.

Leaving the Trust? Your petty cash balance is either paid back to you or deducted from your final pay.

Travelling with petty cash

Using petty cash abroad needs advance approval from your manager and the CEO — record it in rands using the exchange rate at the time.

Petty cash abroad is for work only — no sightseeing or personal shopping — and receipts must be translated into English.

Lost or stolen petty cash abroad? Report it to your manager and, where possible, local authorities — then file a police affidavit on your return.

Do I still need to keep the paper slip?

Yes. Photographing it in the app doesn't replace the original — Finance still needs the physical slip or invoice by the 6th of the month.

What if the AI reads the slip wrong?

It happens with faded or crumpled receipts. Just correct the date, amount, merchant or category before saving — nothing is locked in until you tap Save.

I can't find the right vote number — what do I do?

Search by project name instead of the code. If it's still not there, check with your project manager before guessing — an entry can always be edited later once you have the right vote.

Does saving an entry send it anywhere?

No. Entries stay on your phone until you open a module's Export screen and tap Sync — that's the only step that writes to Google Sheets or uploads receipts to Drive.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Any entries not yet synced are only stored on that device. Sync often, especially after a busy trip, so nothing depends on a single phone.

Can I use this for a personal purchase by mistake?

No — both the credit card and petty cash agreements are clear that personal spend is never permitted, including trips like an Uber from home to your usual work base. Any personal use is treated as a debt owed to WildTrust.

My card was lost or possibly compromised — what first?

Call Investec straight away on 0860 110 161 (+27 11 286 9663 from abroad) to stop the card, then get a police case number and let your finance manager know.

Who do I ask if I'm unsure about a rule?

Your project manager or the finance team — both agreements are reviewed by them, and it's always better to check first than to have a claim rejected later.

I bought a small tool or asset — do I need to do anything else?

Yes — if it's under R5,000 on card or petty cash, let the Procurement Manager know so it's added to the insurance and asset registers.

Is there a limit on how much petty cash I can hold or spend?

Yes — R20,000 a month in draws, and R5,000 per transaction. Anything above that needs your manager's written motivation, reviewed by Finance.
